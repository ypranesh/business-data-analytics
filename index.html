Work

E-commerce · Customer Analytics

The Problem

An e-commerce business had two years of transaction data sitting unused. Marketing budget was being spent equally across all customers — including those who had already left.

The Approach

Analysed 96,000+ customer records to segment the entire base by purchase behaviour. Identified who the best customers are, who is slipping away, and who is already gone.

What You Get

A clear customer map broken into actionable groups — Champions worth 2.3x the average customer, an At Risk group representing 40% of total revenue, and a Promising segment ripe for a first follow-up. Each group with a recommended action.

The Result

Late deliveries proven to increase churn by 21 percentage points. A logistics problem was quietly driving a retention crisis.

Retail · Business Dashboard

The Problem

A retail business had no single view of performance. Decisions were being made from memory and gut feel.

The Approach

Mapped stakeholder requirements across sales, regional, and customer teams. Built a two-dashboard system that everyone from the floor manager to the CEO could read without training.

What You Get

Revenue and profit trends month by month, which regions are profitable and which are bleeding, which products are worth pushing and which are quietly destroying margin, and how customer segments behave differently.

The Result

Discount-driven losses on two product lines identified and quantified for the first time.

Automation · Forecasting

The Problem

Every Monday morning, someone spent two hours pulling numbers from different places, formatting a report, and sending it out. It was manual, error-prone, and took time away from actual decisions.

The Approach

Mapped the entire reporting process, identified every data source, and automated it end to end. The report now builds and delivers itself.

What You Get

A clean weekly PDF landing in your inbox every Monday before you arrive — covering last week's sales performance, trends, and an 8-week forward forecast. No spreadsheets, no manual effort, no waiting.

The Result

A 2-hour weekly task reduced to zero. Forecasting added as a bonus — something the team didn't have before at all.
